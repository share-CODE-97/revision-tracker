Anchor Years

  • 1206 — Qutbuddin Aibak founds the Slave Dynasty (Delhi Sultanate begins).
  • 1290 — Khilji takeover  ·  1320 — Tughlaq takeover.
  • 1398 — Timur's invasion; Tughlaq power collapses.
  • 1414 — Sayyid  ·  1451 — Lodi.
  • 1526 — 1st Battle of Panipat: Babur ends the Sultanate, starts the Mughals.
  • 1540 — Sher Shah defeats Humayun  ·  1555 — Humayun returns.
  • 1556 — 2nd Battle of Panipat: Akbar's reign effectively begins.
  • 1600 — English East India Company chartered (Akbar's reign).
  • 1707 — Aurangzeb dies; Mughal decline sets in.
Chain mnemonic for the five Sultanate dynasties: M-K-T-S-L — Mamluk → Khilji → Tughlaq → Sayyid → Lodi ("My King Took Seven Lands").
Mughal six: B-H-(Sur)-A-J-S-A — Babur, Humayun, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb.
Only woman on the throne: Razia Sultana.   Only dynasty that is an interregnum: Sur.
