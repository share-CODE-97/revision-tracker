Tricks That Kill the Hard Ones

Table Trick
× 9 9n = 10n − n. Also: digits of the answer always add to 9 (9 × 7 = 63 → 6 + 3 = 9).
× 11 Up to 9: repeat the digit (11 × 7 = 77). For 10–99: split the digits and insert their sum (11 × 23 = 2 (2+3) 3 = 253).
× 5 5n = 10n ÷ 2. Always ends in 0 or 5.
× 15 15n = 10n + 5n, or = 30n ÷ 2. 15 × 8 = 240 ÷ 2 = 120.
× 25 25n = 100n ÷ 4. 25 × 7 = 700 ÷ 4 = 175.
× 19 19n = 20n − n. 19 × 6 = 120 − 6 = 114.
× 21 21n = 20n + n. 21 × 7 = 140 + 7 = 147.
× 29 29n = 30n − n. 29 × 8 = 240 − 8 = 232.
Teens 13–19 n × 1m = n × (10 + m) = 10n + nm. Break into a ×10 plus a small table.
Study order: Tables 1–10 must be instant (they are the building blocks). Then learn 11, 12, 15, 20, 25 — they have easy patterns. The rest (13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21–24, 26–29) fall out of the two rules (n+1) = n + 1× and (n−1) = n − 1×. Never memorise blindly what you can derive in one step.

Drill method: read a full column aloud, cover the right column, write answers on paper, check, mark the misses. Repeat only the misses.
