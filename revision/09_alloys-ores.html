Alloys & Ores

Metals · compositions · uses — tap any card to test yourself.

Two ideas, one page. An alloy is a man-made mixture of a metal with other metals (or a non-metal). An ore is a natural mineral from which a metal can be extracted profitably. Everything below is tap-to-reveal — say the answer out loud first, then check.

2 · Alloy patterns that save you

Brass vs Bronze — never mix these up again.
Bronze has Tin → Cu 90 + Sn 10
Brass has Zinc → Cu 70 + Zn 30
  • Copper family. If the biggest share is Cu, it's a copper alloy — Brass, Bronze, Gunmetal, Delta, Muntz, Dutch, Monel, German Silver, Bell Metal, Rolled Gold.
  • Bell Metal is a tin-heavy Bronze. Bronze is 90:10, Bell Metal is 80:20 — "more tin, more ring".
  • Delta Metal is the only copper alloy containing iron → aircraft propeller.
  • Monel Metal = Cu + Ni → strong, corrosion-resistant → container bases.
  • German Silver contains no silver. It's Cu + Zn + Ni.
  • Solder = Pb 50 + Sn 50 → soldering. Equal halves, easy to remember.
  • Rose Metal is the only alloy with bismuth → lowest melting point → automatic fuses.
  • Type Metal is mostly lead (80%) → printing type.
  • The two Al alloys: Magnalium (Al 95 + Mg 5, aircraft body) and Duralumin (Al 94 + Cu 3 + Mg 2 + Mn 1, utensils + aircraft).
  • Stainless Steel = Fe 75 / Cr 15 / Ni 9.5 / C 0.05. Remember "75-15-9.5".
  • Nickel Steel = Fe 95 + Ni 5 → wires and vehicle parts.

4 · Ore patterns — read the formula, name the ore

  • Pyrite = sulphur. Every ore ending in -pyrite has an S in it: Iron pyrite FeS₂, Copper pyrite CuFeS₂, Arsenopyrite FeAsS.
  • Sulphide ores (look for S alone): Galena PbS, Cinnabar HgS, Zinc blende ZnS, Argentite Ag₂S, Greenockite CdS, Stibnite Sb₂S₃, Chalcocite Cu₂S.
  • Carbonate ores (look for CO₃): Magnesite, Siderite, Calamine, Cerussite, Strontianite, Calcite, Malachite, Azurite.
  • Sulphate ores (look for SO₄): Gypsum, Epsomite, Barium sulphate, Sodium sulphate.
  • All-aluminium club: Bauxite, Cryolite, Corundum, Diaspore.
  • Magnesium club: Magnesite, Dolomite, Carnallite, Epsomite.
  • Shared ores: Carnallite belongs to both K and Mg; Dolomite belongs to both Ca and Mg.
  • Zinc quartet: Blende (S), Franklinite (Fe), Calamine (CO₃), Zincite (O).
  • Iron quartet: Hematite Fe₂O₃, Magnetite Fe₃O₄, Siderite FeCO₃, Iron pyrite FeS₂ — "H-M-S-P".
  • Lead trio in order: Galena (sulphide) → Cerussite (carbonate) → Cotunnite (chloride).
  • Horn silver is the nickname of AgCl; silver glance is Ag₂S.

Which alloy is used for bells and statues?

Bell Metal — Cu 80% + Sn 20%

Brass is made of which two metals, and in what ratio?

Cu 70% + Zn 30%

Which aluminium alloy is used for aircraft bodies?

Magnalium — Al 95% + Mg 5% (Duralumin is also used in aircraft).

Which low-melting alloy is used in automatic fuses?

Rose Metal — Bi 50% + Pb 28% + Sn 22%

Which alloy is used in the printing industry, and what is its main metal?

Type Metal — Pb 80% + Sb 15% + Sn 5% (lead).

Which alloy is used to join metals together?

Solder — Pb 50% + Sn 50%

Name the sulphide ore of mercury.

Cinnabar — HgS

Which iron ore has the formula Fe₃O₄?

Magnetite

What is the main ore of aluminium?

Bauxite — Al₂O₃·2H₂O

Galena is the ore of which metal, and what is its formula?

Lead — PbS
