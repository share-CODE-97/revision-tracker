Anchor Years — Lock These

  • 1556 — Second Battle of Panipat (Bairam Khan vs Hemu). Akbar's reign effectively begins.
  • 1561 — Malwa annexed  ·  1564 — Gondwana (Rani Durgavati).
  • 1567–68 — Chittorgarh  ·  1576 — Haldighati.
  • 1572–73 — Gujarat  ·  1576 — Bengal (Rajmahal)  ·  1592 — Orissa.
  • 1581 — Kabul  ·  1586 — Kashmir  ·  1595 — Kandahar + Balochistan.
  • 1601 — Asirgarh falls; Khandesh annexed — Akbar's last major conquest.

BPSC / Exam Focus — High-Yield Facts

Fact Why it matters
Battle of Tukaroi (1575) + Treaty of Katak Treaty is a classic MCQ — Daud Khan accepted Mughal suzerainty; broke it → Rajmahal (1576).
Battle of Rajmahal (1576) End of the Karrani (Afghan) dynasty in Bengal — Bengal formally a Mughal Subah.
Battle of Haldighati (1576) Man Singh commanded Mughals (Akbar did NOT personally lead). Result — tactical Mughal win, strategic stalemate; Mewar never fully annexed.
Siege of Chittor (1567–68) Jaimal and Patta — iconic Rajput resistance; Udai Singh II (Rana Pratap's father) had fled to Udaipur.
Rani Durgavati — Narrai (1564) Led by Asaf Khan; Durgavati died fighting — often asked as "who resisted Akbar in Gondwana".
Chand Bibi — Ahmadnagar (1595–1600) Regent who defended Ahmadnagar; Mughals took the fort only after her death (1600).
Asirgarh (1601) Akbar's last major conquest; Khandesh annexed as Subah "Dandesh".
Kabul (1581) Against his own half-brother Mirza Hakim — internal consolidation, not external conquest.
Region sweep mnemonic: N → R → C → E → W → S — North (Panipat) → Rajputana (Chittor, Haldighati) → Central (Malwa, Gondwana) → East (Bengal, Orissa) → West (Gujarat, Sindh) → South (Deccan).
Only three campaigns where Akbar personally commanded: Chittor (1567), Gujarat (1572), Asirgarh (1599–1601).
Haldighati ≠ Rana Pratap's surrender. After 1576, Pratap waged guerrilla war and recovered most of Mewar by 1585 — examiners test this.
Diplomacy > battles in Rajputana: Amer (1562), Jaisalmer (1570–71), Bikaner (1570) were submission/alliance — NOT fought.
